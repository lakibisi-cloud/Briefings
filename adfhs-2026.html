A 24-month research programme on heart and metabolic health. The first study follows adults with obesity and high blood pressure for six months, tracking weight, waist, blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar and sleep alongside WHOOP coaching.

DoH × Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi × AstraZeneca

Agreement

Makes Abu Dhabi a priority location in AstraZeneca's clinical trial network. Explores the feasibility of up to 30 trials by 2030 in cardiovascular, renal and metabolic disease, with an estimated AED180 million (US$50 million) research investment. The first six trials are expected at CCAD by the end of 2026.

The Future of Healthy Living: A $16.4 Trillion Opportunity

Report

White paper with the McKinsey Health Institute and DoH's Healthy Living programme, released on the sidelines of UNGA ahead of the summit. Estimates that removing behavioural, metabolic and environmental risk factors could add US$16.4 trillion to annual global GDP in 2050 (about 9%), avert 1.5 billion DALYs (about 47% of projected disease burden) and add up to 12 years of healthy life expectancy. Notes that 19 of 23 major drivers of health sit largely outside the formal healthcare system.
